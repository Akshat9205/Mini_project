The Challenge Students Face

Why We Built This

In today’s world, it has become a major challenge for students to consistently improve their skills. Many want to learn competitive programming but struggle to stay motivated for daily practice.

They set goals, but tracking them and maintaining motivation becomes difficult. Traditional to-do lists often feel boring and fail to keep students engaged.

The result? Goals get started but remain incomplete, leading to lost progress and demotivation.

😩

The struggle is real...

Our Solution – Skill Progress Tracker

A platform where students can set their learning goals, earn XP points, unlock badges, and track their rank on the leaderboard.

Transform Learning into a Game

Our platform makes skill development addictive by combining learning with gamification. Set goals, earn rewards, and watch yourself grow like never before.
